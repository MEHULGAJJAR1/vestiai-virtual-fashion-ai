Upload a product photo of a t-shirt, shirt, jacket, kurta or dress. VestiAI removes the background, builds a garment mask, classifies the category and then either tracks it onto your body in real time (pose landmarks → affine/perspective warp) or synthesises a photorealistic try-on with a fine-tuned diffusion model.

Two pipelines, one UI

webcam→ BlazePose 33 landmarks→ smoothed anchors→ warp + occlusion→ composite

Fast mode runs entirely from pose geometry: 10–30 ms per frame on a laptop CPU, no GPU needed. Texture and transparency are preserved exactly; fabric folds and lighting are not synthesised.

AI mode calls your fine-tuned diffusion model at a throttled interval (default every 700 ms) and reuses the cached frame in between, so the live view stays fluid and the garment never flickers.

Train it on your own data

VestiAI ships a real training pipeline: dataset preparation (VITON-HD / DressCode layout), augmentation, AMP, gradient accumulation and checkpointing, validation with generated samples, TensorBoard/W&B metrics, evaluation metrics and export — all runnable from the AI Training page or the CLI. 

python scripts/train.py --mode QUICK_DEMO     # verify (minutes)
python scripts/train.py --mode FINE_TUNE      # adapt on your GPU
python scripts/evaluate.py --checkpoint checkpoints/best_model

Preprocessing pipeline

  • ①Decode + EXIF fix, reject < 160 px
  • ②Background removal (rembg if installed, else GrabCut)
  • ③Alpha refinement + largest-component keep
  • ④Quality gate: skin-ratio, aspect, coverage
  • ⑤Category classification (CLIP → head → rules)
  • ⑥Bbox crop → 768 px square canvas + mask
  • ⑦Palette extraction + closet record

Workflow

  1. Upload a garment product photo → preprocessing runs automatically.
  2. Select it in Live Try-On and press Start Camera.
  3. Move around: the garment follows your shoulders, torso and arms.
  4. Leave the frame → the garment hides; return → it comes back.
  5. Toggle AI mode for diffusion-quality frames at a throttled rate.
  6. Capture a photo or record a clip to keep the result.
